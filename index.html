The bronze they set on the lawn

Washington puts memory in bronze. This one was planted in public, aimed at a war he never had to fight.

National Mall

The statue went up on the National Mall to mock Trump's Vietnam draft deferment. Bone spurs. A war other people fought. A grin cast in metal and left on the country's front lawn, where monuments are supposed to behave.

Denald Tremp is that face, given a name you can say out loud. The symbol is $TREMP. Solana holds the coin. The Mall held the joke. Neither one blinks.

Pagodas in the dusk, a reflecting pool, an obelisk at the end of the water. The landscape remembers a war. The statue remembers who stayed home. $TREMP is the plaque under both.
